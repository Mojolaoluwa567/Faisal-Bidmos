About Us

Our understanding of customer satisfaction is that our services should surpass the expectation of the customer in terms of value creation, delivering on timelines and increasing stakeholder’s returns.

Vision

To be a foremost financial consulting boutique in Sub-Sahara Africa.

Mission

To mirror International best practice in the provision of our consulting services drawing from our pedigree and scores of years of experience in banking, finance, accounting services, regulatory compliance and enterprise risk management.

Our History

The audit and accounting firm FAISAL BIDMOS AND CO was established to premium audit and assurance services but has since grown to provide other range of services leveraging the experience of the partners in banking, tax, consulting and other finance related areas.

We are known for providing quality services, which reflect the high standards we demand of ourselves. Our dedication to the three underlying principles of professionalism, responsiveness, and quality, differentiate us from the rest. Our staff and management are professionals and are known to be people of integrity.
