Meet the Team

Our dynamic team combines a wealth of experience and a passion for excellence. We work together to provide tailored solutions that drive success and ensure your business’s financial health and transparency.

Mr Faisal Bidmos FCA

Team Lead / Managing Consultant

Faisal, born on 16 February 1972, graduated from the University of Lagos with a degree in Mathematics and Statistics and later earned a Diploma in Computer Science and an MBA in Finance. He became a Chartered Accountant and a Fellow of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nigeria. He worked in commercial banking for 14 years, holding senior roles at Equity Bank of Nigeria Limited, Intercontinental Bank PLC, and Access Bank Plc.

Mr Opeyemi Ogundele FCA

Associate Consultant

Opeyemi Ogundele is a Fellow of ICAN with over 18 years of experience in auditing, finance, and training. He has served in leadership roles at John O.G. Group, Foodco, and various audit firms. He also coordinates ICAN tuition at Uplink Professionals and runs his own firm, Moses Ogundele & Co. He is active in Christian missions and public speaking.

Mr Ganiu Muniru

Peer Review Partner

Mr. Muniru is an expert auditor for U.S. government-funded projects including those by USAID, UNICEF, and USCDC. With over two decades of experience, he has led audits for FHI 360, NEDC, and Catholic Caritas. He is an IFRS expert and chairman of the IFRS Implementation Team at Parker Randall Nigeria. He studied at the Polytechnic Ibadan and is a chartered accountant.

Ayodele Lasisi ACA

Mr Olatunde Ibrahim ACA

Mr Faisal Bidmos FCA

Team Lead / Managing Consultant

Faisal was born on 16th February 1972 and attended the prestigious University of Lagos, Nigeria, where he graduated with a B.Sc (Hons) in Mathematics and Statistics in November 1995. He received the 'Best Student, 1990 Year 1 Set, Department of Mathematics, Faculty of Science' scholarship award in November 1991. He also obtained a Diploma in Computer Science and an MBA in Finance from the same University in 1997 and 1999 respectively.

Faisal qualified as a Chartered Accountant (ACA) in November 2008 and became a Fellow of ICAN in April 2020. He is also an associate member of the Chartered Institute of Taxation of Nigeria (CITN).

He spent fourteen (14) active and meritorious years in commercial banking, starting with Equity Bank of Nigeria Limited in June 1997 as an Executive Trainee at the Allen Avenue Branch. Through exemplary hard work, he rose to become Senior Manager in July 2011 with Access Bank Plc. He won the 'Best Staff' award twice at Equity Bank for outstanding performance in the Financial Control department.

He later became Head of MIS/Planning at both Equity Bank and Intercontinental Bank PLC, Lagos. He led the committee that implemented the upgrade of the Phoenix Banking System to the Equinox System, and played a key role in restructuring the conventional branch-based business model into a sectoral model in May 2005.

He worked as an audit trainee and later Audit Senior at Lukman Akande & Co and Kayode Adewuyi & Co over 4 years. He has consulted for many companies on financial management, forensic audit, and business process engineering. In 2015, he converted many MFB financial statements from nGAAP to IFRS. He is an expert in Internal Control and ICFR with knowledge of COSO 16 Protocols.

He has extensive teaching experience in Strategic Planning, Financial Accounting, and MIS. He taught at the Distance Learning Institute (DLI) and Mayo Associates in Lagos, and served as a project supervisor and examiner at the University of Lagos (2016).

He authored 'Understanding Computer Applications and Management Information System' for tertiary institutions in 2004. He is widely travelled and happily married.
